1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device (computer, mobile or tablet) when you visit them. They serve to remember your preferences, improve your browsing experience and, in some cases, collect statistical information.

2. What cookies does this website use?

Currently, this website is a static site that does not use any first-party or third-party cookies.

No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies are installed. Google Analytics or similar tools are not used.

3. Third-party cookies
