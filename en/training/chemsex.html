Chemsex: understanding to intervene

Specialised training programme

A comprehensive programme for professionals who want to understand the Chemsex phenomenon and acquire effective intervention tools. Each module combines scientific evidence, clinical experience and practical resources applicable from day one.

For healthcare professionals

Sexology and Chemsex

"Full sexuality beyond substances"

How to reconnect with desire and pleasure when substances have mediated the sexual experience for so long?

⏱ 120 min📋 Training talk + clinical cases

Group Intervention in Chemsex

"The group as a therapeutic space"

Group intervention is one of the most effective tools in addressing Chemsex. How to design, facilitate and sustain it?

⏱ 120 min📋 Training workshop + role-playing

Peer Programme in Chemsex

"Those who have lived it can accompany"

A network of trained peers can multiply the reach of any intervention. At PVB IS QUEER we built a community of ~200 people in one year.

⏱ 120 min📋 Training talk + replicable model

Trauma and Chemsex

"Behind consumption, there is often a wound"

Sexual assault, intimate partner violence, complex trauma... Chemsex and trauma feed each other. How to intervene without retraumatising?

⏱ 120 min📋 Training talk + clinical vignettes

Prevention in Schools

"Before the first use, there is a conversation pending"

Inclusive sex education and early awareness are the best prevention tools. How to bring this message to young people?

⏱ 120 min📋 Training talk + adapted materials

Families and Close Circle

"When someone close practises Chemsex, you also need support"

Families and partners of Chemsex users suffer in silence. How to support them? What do they need to know? How can they help without harming themselves?

⏱ 120 min📋 Training talk + practical guide

Harm Reduction in Chemsex

"Not everything is abstinence: strategies that save lives"

Not all users want or can stop Chemsex immediately. Harm reduction offers practical strategies to minimise risks and preserve health.

⏱ 120 min📋 Training workshop + practical protocols
