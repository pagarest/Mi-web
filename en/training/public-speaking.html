Public speaking fundamentals

Progressive applied public speaking programme

Public speaking is not an innate talent. It's a skill you train. This programme takes you step by step from basics to advanced: structuring a clear message, connecting with the audience, using your body and voice as tools, and being able to persuade and inspire.

Each module is practical, with real exercises and personalised feedback. Designed so every participant finishes speaking better than they thought possible.

Progressive modules

1. Your first time in front of an audience

"The first step is daring to speak"

Overcome the fear of speaking in front of others. Discover your strengths as a communicator and the areas to work on. A first short speech about yourself.

⏱ 90 min📋 Practical exercise + individual feedback

2. Building a clear message

"Without structure, there's no clarity"

Learn to organise ideas with opening, body and closing. Organisation methods depending on your objective. How to select and prioritise content.

⏱ 120 min📋 Workshop + structured speech preparation

3. Knowing where you're headed

"If you don't know where you're going, neither does the audience"

Define a clear objective for every intervention. Align structure, content and delivery with that purpose. Convey conviction and confidence.

⏱ 120 min📋 Workshop + purpose-driven speech

4. Choosing the words that matter

"It's not just what you say, but how you say it"

Choosing the right words for each audience. Vivid, concrete and memorable language. Eliminating filler words and language that weakens the message.

⏱ 120 min📋 Rewriting exercises + presentation

5. What you say without speaking

"The audience listens with their eyes before their ears"

Posture, gestures, eye contact, movement in space. How to use body language to reinforce the message and project confidence.

⏱ 120 min📋 Workshop + video recording and analysis

6. Your voice as an instrument

"A monotone voice loses any audience"

Pace, volume, pauses, tone and emphasis. How to use your voice as an instrument to maintain attention and convey emotion.

⏱ 120 min📋 Vocal exercises + expressive reading

7. Building arguments that convince

"Data without story doesn't convince; story without data doesn't hold"

How to research, select data and build solid arguments. Integrating evidence with narrative to generate credibility and drive action.

⏱ 120 min📋 Workshop + data-driven persuasive speech

8. Moving something in those who listen

"The speech that moves something inside those who hear it"

How to connect emotionally, tell stories that resonate and close with a call to action. The culminating module of the programme.

⏱ 120 min📋 Final speech + group feedback
