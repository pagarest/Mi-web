Communication that connects

Training programme for teams and organisations

Communication is the invisible infrastructure of any team. When it works, everything flows. When it fails, everything suffers: motivation, trust, results.

This programme is designed for teams that want to communicate with greater clarity, manage conflicts better and generate real impact in every interaction. Each module is independent and adapts to your organisation's specific needs.

Available modules

Communication within the team

"Talking should be more than just informing"

How to build communication dynamics that generate trust, speed up decisions and reduce misunderstandings. Active listening, effective feedback and difficult conversations.

⏱ 120 min 📋 Practical workshop + group dynamics

Conflict resolution

"Conflict isn't the problem"

Tools to address tensions constructively. How to de-escalate, find agreements and turn conflict into an opportunity for improvement.

⏱ 120 min 📋 Practical workshop + role-playing

High-impact presentations

"Make your message land, be understood and be remembered"

How to structure, design and deliver presentations that connect with the audience. Storytelling, body language and managing nerves.

⏱ 120 min 📋 Practical workshop + live presentations

Customer-focused communication

"Understand before being understood"

How to adapt your message to your audience, manage expectations and build trust with internal and external customers.

⏱ 120 min 📋 Practical workshop + real cases

Communicating vision and strategy

"If the team doesn't understand the why, they'll hardly follow the how"

How to convey vision, objectives and organisational changes clearly, honestly and motivationally. For leaders and middle managers.

⏱ 120 min 📋 Practical workshop + communication plan

Nonviolent Communication

"Say what you need without attacking, listen without defending"

Based on Marshall Rosenberg's model. Learn to observe without judging, identify needs, express clear requests and receive difficult messages without reactivity.

⏱ 180 min 📋 Practical workshop + pair exercises
