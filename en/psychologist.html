Why psychology

I chose this profession because I believe no one should feel judged for what they feel, for what they've been through, or for how they've chosen to live. My practice is a space where there are no right answers and no wrong lives.

I also believe that no two people are the same, and therefore no two therapies should be the same. What works for one person may not work for another. My job is to listen to you, understand your story, and build a path together that makes sense for you right now.

How I work

Every person arrives with their own story, their own pace and their own needs. My job is to adapt to you, not the other way around.

No judgement

What you tell me stays here. There's nothing you could say that would make me see you differently. Not your orientation, not your practices, not your choices. You come as you are.

Radical respect

Your life is yours. I'm not here to tell you how to live it. I'm here to help you live it with less pain and more freedom.

Personalised approach

Sometimes we need to go slowly. Sometimes you're ready to go deep. You set the pace.

Closeness

I'm not a professional behind a barrier. I'm a person who has chosen to be present with you in difficult moments. With honesty, with warmth, with commitment.

What I can help you with

I've trained and specialised in these areas:

EMDR. Reprocessing traumatic experiences. Deep change that you feel, not just understand.
Chemsex. Support from a harm reduction perspective. No moralising. Working with you, not against you.
Emotional regulation. For when your emotions overwhelm you and you can't find the brake.
LGBTQIA+ community. A safe space where your identity and desire are not questioned.
Trauma. What happened still weighs on you. We can work so it stops running your present.
